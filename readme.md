# 🐶 Cat Person vs. Dog Person Quiz 🐱

A fun, smart, and highly accurate quiz highlighting the hilarious differences between living with our favorite furry companions!

---

### 1. The Hierarchy

**Question:** What is the fundamental difference in the human-pet hierarchy?  
**Answer:** Dogs have masters. Cats have staff.

### 2. The $50 Toy Test

**Question:** If you buy a state-of-the-art $50 interactive pet toy, how will the two animals react?  
**Answer:** The dog will destroy the toy in exactly five minutes. The cat will ignore the toy completely and play with the crinkly receipt it came with.

### 3. The 3:00 AM Alarm

**Question:** What does a sudden noise from your pet at 3:00 AM mean?  
**Answer:** A bark means "INTRUDER ALERT!" (which is usually just a leaf blowing outside). A meow means "I can see the bottom of my food bowl and I am currently starving to death."

### 4. The Rainy Sunday

**Question:** How do dog people and cat people experience a rainy Sunday morning?  
**Answer:** The dog person is outside in a raincoat holding a plastic bag; the cat person is trapped under a sleeping feline, legally unable to reach their coffee.

### 5. The Vacation Guilt Trip

**Question:** How do dogs and cats make you feel guilty when you pack a suitcase for a vacation?  
**Answer:** Dogs give you the ultimate sad, trembling puppy-dog eyes. Cats actively ignore you, shed on your clean black clothes, and pack themselves into your suitcase to prove they are an inconvenience.
